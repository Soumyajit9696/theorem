01Highlights

Everything it does

01

Live typesetting

MathJax renders as you type, with full environment support — equation, align, cases, split, gather, matrices, arrays. Milliseconds, not seconds.

02

Every export you need

Transparent PNG, compact WebP, JPG, standalone vector SVG, and true vector PDF — at up to 6× resolution with a live pixel readout before you commit.

03

Paste & drag anywhere

Copy as a paste-ready image straight into Word, Docs, and Outlook; on the desktop, drag the equation out of the window into PowerPoint or a folder as a file.

04

Smart PNGs

Every exported PNG carries its LaTeX source inside the file. Drop it back into Theorem — on any machine — and the editable formula pops back out.

05

A full toolbox

Inline autocomplete, 40+ formula templates, a 160-command reference, and a symbol palette — with placeholders selected, ready to type over.

06

Total visual control

Ink color, size, background, padding, rounded corners, a separate color for highlighting parts of an equation, and a dark theme throughout.

07

Private by design

100% client-side. Nothing you type ever leaves your machine — and the desktop edition adds full offline.

08

Stay in the flow

Open and drag in .tex files, share links that carry the equation in the URL, keep a history of recent renders, and pick up where you left off.

02Workflow

Three steps, ten seconds

01

Type

Write or paste LaTeX. Autocomplete, the palette, and templates fill the rest — errors show inline, in plain language.

02

Style

Set the ink, background, padding, and corners on a live checkerboard stage that shows exactly what will be exported.

03

Export

PNG, SVG, WebP, JPG, or PDF — or one keystroke to the clipboard, one drag to your slide deck.
