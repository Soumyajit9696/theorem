Built for people who write math

Everything a LaTeX to image converter should do, without an upload step or an account.

Live typesetting
MathJax renders as you type, with full environment support: equation, align, cases, split, gather, matrices and arrays. Errors show inline in plain language.
Paste and drag anywhere
Copy the equation as an image straight into Word, Docs and Outlook. On the desktop, drag it out of the window into PowerPoint or a folder as a file.
Editable PNGs
Every exported PNG carries its LaTeX source inside the file. Drop it back into Theorem on any machine and the editable formula returns.
A full toolbox
Inline autocomplete, 40+ formula templates, a 160-command reference and a symbol palette, with placeholders selected and ready to type over.
Control over the look
Set ink colour, size, background, padding and rounded corners, pick a separate colour to highlight part of an equation, and switch to a dark theme.
Private by design
Rendering is 100% client-side, so your equations are never uploaded. The desktop app adds full offline use.
Pick up where you left off
Open or drag in .tex files, share a link that carries the equation in its URL, and return to your recent renders from the history.

Questions people ask

How do I convert LaTeX to PNG?

Type or paste your LaTeX into Theorem, check the live preview, choose PNG and export. The background stays transparent unless you pick a colour.

Is Theorem free?

Yes. The web app and the desktop app are free and open source under the MIT license, with no account and no watermark.

Does Theorem upload my equations?

No. Rendering happens entirely in your browser or in the desktop app, so what you type never leaves your device.

Can I export a LaTeX equation with a transparent background?

Yes. PNG, WebP and SVG exports keep a transparent background. JPG cannot store transparency, so it uses the background colour you choose.

Does Theorem work offline?

The desktop app works fully offline. The web app also keeps working offline after your first visit.

Which LaTeX does Theorem support?

Theorem typesets math with MathJax, including equation, align, cases, split, gather, matrices and arrays. It renders equations, not whole documents with a documentclass.

Can I edit an exported equation later?

Yes. Every PNG exported from Theorem carries its LaTeX source inside the file. Drop the PNG back into Theorem on any machine and the editable formula returns.
